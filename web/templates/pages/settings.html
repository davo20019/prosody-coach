About prosody CLI: prosody info

Pitch (intonation). The highness or lowness of your voice. English uses wide pitch variation to convey meaning and emotion. Target: 100–150 Hz variation range.

Volume (stress). Loudness variation between stressed and unstressed syllables. English emphasizes important words by making them louder. Target: 6–10 dB contrast.

Tempo (speed). Speaking rate and its variation. Target: 130–160 WPM with 15–25% variation.

Rhythm (timing pattern). The timing between syllables. Spanish is syllable-timed; English is stress-timed. Target: PVI 55–65 (higher = more English-like).

Pauses (strategic silence). Deliberate breaks for emphasis and breathing. Target: 3–5 pauses per 30 seconds, 0.5–1.5 s each.
